Redressal Process

How Complaints Are Resolved

An end-to-end transparent workflow ensuring every student complaint is recorded, audited, and addressed by authorized division staff.

01

1. Lodge Grievance

Log in to the student dashboard, select the affected campus facility, provide building/room coordinates, attach optional photo evidence, and receive a permanent unique tracking ID.

02

2. Administrative Triage

The Campus Dean or department heads review incoming complaints, assign maintenance engineers (e.g. NOC, Plumbing, Electrical, Estate), and mark the status as "In Progress".

03

3. Action Taken & Resolution

Technicians rectify the issue on-site, record official resolution remarks, and close the complaint as "Resolved". The student receives an instant dashboard notification with the full report.
